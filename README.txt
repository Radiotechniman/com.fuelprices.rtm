Track fuel prices at the pump, either for your area or for a specific station.

DEVICES

Fuel price (area)
Add one device per fuel type (for example Euro 95 or Diesel) for an address of your choice and a search radius. The device shows the lowest price in that area, together with the cheapest station, its address and distance, the average price, the number of stations, the change over the last day and week, and the lowest and highest price ever seen by the device.

Fuel price (station)
Add one device per fuel station. The device shows the price of every fuel the station sells, the lowest and highest price ever seen per fuel, the address and, when known, whether the station is open.

FLOW CARDS

Area device
- When the price has changed
- When the price drops below a price
- When a new lowest price ever is reached
- When the cheapest station has changed
- The price is (not) below a price
- Refresh data

Station device
- When the price of a fuel has changed
- When the price of a fuel drops below a price
- The price of a fuel is (not) below a price
- Refresh data

SETTINGS

Each device has its own settings: the update interval (5 to 1440 minutes) and a switch to turn automatic refreshing off. With automatic refreshing off, prices are only updated by the "Refresh data" flow card. The area device also has a search radius.

Choose which price is shown under the device name on the tile in the device settings of the Homey app.

DATA

Prices and station information come from the ANWB fuel price service. Addresses are looked up with the PDOK Locatieserver. The prices are not guaranteed to be up to date or correct, so check the price at the pump. Price history, and the lowest and highest price ever, are kept by the app on your Homey and only reach back as far as the device has been added.
